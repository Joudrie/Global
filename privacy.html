Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Globalio (“we”, “the app”) is a free flags & geography game. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. We aim to collect as little as possible.

Who is responsible

Globalio is run by Sean Joudrie, an individual developer based in the United States, who is the data controller for this site. Contact: sjoudrie@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Game progress, stored on your device. Your streaks, scores, learned flags, achievements, settings, profile picture, banner and bio, and similar progress are saved locally in your browser (using localStorage). Unless you make an account, this data stays on your device and is not sent to us. It is needed for the game to work, so it does not require consent.
  • An account, only if you make one. You can play without one. If you sign in to keep your progress on every device, we store your email address and your game progress; see Accounts below.
  • Basic technical data. Like any website, our host (Netlify) processes standard request information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver and secure the site. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser fetches them. Flag images are served from Globalio’s own site; if a country flag fails to load, your browser may fetch a backup copy from flagcdn.com or the jsDelivr CDN, which also see your IP address. The Atlas game loads its historical map scans directly from the Library of Congress (tile.loc.gov) and the David Rumsey Map Collection (davidrumsey.com), which receive your IP address when your browser fetches them.

Accounts

Accounts are optional and free. You must be 13 or older to make one. An account keeps your progress the same on every device you sign in on.

  • What we store. Your email address; your game progress (a copy of the streaks, scores, learned flags, achievements, profile choices and settings saved on your device, so your devices can share them); when your account was made and when you last signed in; if you claim a username, your profile (see Public profiles) and your friends and friend requests (see Friends); and, if you support Globalio, that your account is a Supporter. Supabase also keeps short-lived security logs of sign-ins, which include your IP address.
  • Signing in with Google. If you choose “Continue with Google”, Google confirms who you are and shares your email address, name and profile picture link with us. They are stored with your sign-in details; we only use your email. We never see your Google password. The “Continue with Google” button itself is loaded from Google (accounts.google.com) only when the account section is on screen, so Google may see that visit; Google’s own privacy policy covers that and your Google account.
  • Signing in by email. If you ask for a sign-in link, we email it to you from noreply@globalio.app, sent through our email provider Resend (USA), which processes your email address only to deliver that message. We use your email address only to sign you in and, if you write to us, to reply.
  • Who stores it. Accounts run on Supabase (Supabase, Inc., USA), which stores this data for us as our processor. Only you can read or change your progress through the app; we can see it to keep the service running and to help when you ask.
  • What we don’t do. We never sell your account data, never use it for advertising, and never share it with advertisers. Ads on Globalio don’t know whether you have an account.
  • Why. We use your account data to provide the account you asked for (performing our agreement with you).
  • Deleting it. In the app, go to Settings → Your progress → Delete account. That deletes your account, your email address, your profile and username, your friends and friend requests, and the progress saved in it straight away; the progress on your device stays until you clear it. Signing out keeps your account. You can also email us and we will delete it for you. Supabase’s security logs expire on their own.

Public profiles

A profile shows your stats in one place. Anyone can see their own on the You tab without an account. If you have an account you can also claim a username, which makes a public profile that other players can look up. It is optional, and there are no followers, messages, comments or likes. You can add friends, who can see a little more (see Friends).

  • What is public. Your username; your picture (a country flag or one of your own Flag Studio designs); your banner and bio; your badges (any badge we give you, and up to three achievements you pin); your stats (flags learned, crowns, streaks, games played, best scores, achievements with the day you unlocked them, and the month you started playing); and the Flag Studio designs you choose to show, without their names. Never your email address, your account ID or the rest of your progress.
  • Who can see it. Anyone, signed in or not, can search usernames by their first letters and open a public profile, or open a link to it (globalio.app/?u=yourname). Your stats are what your own app reports; nothing ranks players against each other.
  • Public or private. A profile is public once you claim a username. Turn off “Public profile” on the You tab at any time: then nobody else can find it or open its link, except your friends. You can also change your username there.
  • Hiding and removing. We may hide or rename a profile, or remove a bio, that breaks the terms. Deleting your account deletes your profile straight away. To ask us to hide or remove yours, email sjoudrie@gmail.com.
  • Where it’s kept. With your account on Supabase, until you delete your account.

Friends

If you have an account and a username, you can add friends. A friendship is mutual: you send a request, and you are friends only once the other player accepts. There are no followers, and there are no messages between friends.

  • What friends see. Your friends can see your profile and stats (flags learned, crowns, streaks, best scores, achievements) even when your profile is private, so you can compare scores. They never see your email address, your account ID or the rest of your progress.
  • Who sees your friends. Your friend list and your requests are shown only to you. A friendship or a request is visible only to the two players in it: nobody else, signed in or not, can see who your friends are.
  • Requests. A request shows the player you ask your username, picture and badge. If you ask a player whose profile is private, you see only their username until they accept. To stop spam, we note when each request was sent and keep that note for 30 days: you can send up to 30 requests a day and ask the same player up to 3 times in 30 days.
  • Ending a friendship. Decline a request, cancel your own, or remove a friend at any time, on their profile or on the You tab. The other player isn’t told. A profile we hide for breaking the terms can’t send or get requests and drops out of its friends’ lists.
  • Where it’s kept. With your account on Supabase, until you remove the friend or delete your account (which deletes your friendships and requests straight away).

Analytics

We may measure how Globalio is used (pages viewed, games played, rough location by country, device type) so we can improve it. When measurement is switched on, we use one or both of:

  • GoatCounter counts page views, and which games are opened, without cookies and without storing your IP address. A game open is counted as just the game’s name; it is never linked to you or your account.
  • Google Analytics 4 uses cookies to measure visits and, if you arrived from one of our ads, which ad brought you. See how Google uses this information.

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google’s consent message asks for your choice before any advertising or analytics cookies are set. Until you answer, those cookies stay off. You can change your choice at any time from the “Privacy and cookie settings” link that Google shows on the page.

Advertising

Globalio may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google’s Ads Settings.
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • For more on third-party cookies and opting out, see aboutads.info and youronlinechoices.eu.

Why we use your data (legal bases)

  • Delivering and securing the site (hosting, request logs): our legitimate interest in running a working, safe website.
  • Cookie-free page counts (GoatCounter): our legitimate interest in understanding which pages and games are used. No personal profile is built.
  • Accounts (Supabase, and Google if you sign in with it): to provide the account you asked for.
  • Advertising and analytics cookies (Google AdSense, Google Analytics): your consent where the law requires it, including in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. You can withdraw it at any time.

How long data is kept

  • Game progress stays in your browser until you clear it.
  • Account data (email, progress, profile, friends, sign-in times) is kept until you delete your account. Notes of when friend requests were sent are kept for 30 days.
  • Google Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months.
  • Hosting request logs are kept by Netlify for a short period for security and are not used to identify you.
  • Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer them.

Where data is processed

Our providers (Netlify, Supabase, Resend, Google, GoatCounter and the flag image CDNs above) may process data in the United States and other countries. Where data leaves the EEA, UK or Switzerland, these providers rely on recognised safeguards such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, to object to or restrict its use, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier use. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you can also complain to your local data protection authority. If you have an account, you can delete it yourself in Settings, or email us for a copy of what it holds. Without one, we hold no profile of you, and most data about you is held by the providers above; email us and we will help with any request.

US state privacy rights

Residents of California and other US states with privacy laws may opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising. Google’s US privacy message on this site provides that choice. We do not sell personal information for money.

Images and third-party content

Flag and emblem images are sourced from Wikimedia Commons and are used under their respective licenses; Globalio serves its own copies. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Children

Globalio is an educational game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We don’t ask anyone for personal information, and accounts are only for people 13 or older. In the EEA, visitors under 16 (or the lower age set by their country) should ask a parent before accepting advertising cookies. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can clear all locally-stored game data at any time by clearing your browser’s site data for Globalio. If you have an account, Delete account in Settings removes it and the progress saved in it. You can manage or block cookies through your browser settings.
