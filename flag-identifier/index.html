Flag identifier

Saw a flag and don't know whose it is? Describe it: its layout, its colours and anything on it, like a star or a sun. Globalio lists every flag that fits, best match first: countries, US states and provinces, cities, historical flags, pride flags, regional movements and more.

Identify a flag

How it works

1 · KindA country, a state, a city, a pride flag… or not sure
2 · ShapeStripes, a cross, a triangle at the pole…
3 · ColoursEvery colour you remember
4 · DetailsStars, a sun, a coat of arms, a fleur-de-lis, writing
5 · MatchesEvery flag that fits, best first

It ranks rather than filters, so one colour remembered wrong doesn't hide the answer: that flag moves to a list of close matches instead. You can open the list after any question and change any answer.

Questions

How does it work?

Answer up to four questions: what kind of flag you think it is, its layout, its colours and anything on it, like stars, a sun or a coat of arms. Every flag is ranked by how well it fits, and the list is there at every step.

What if I remember a colour wrong?

It still shows up. Flags are ranked, not filtered: one wrong colour or detail moves a flag into the "close" list instead of hiding it, and light blue for blue only counts half.

Which flags can it find?

3,618 of the 5,000+ flags in the Codex so far: all 197 country flags, plus 3,421 more: US states, Canadian provinces, Japanese prefectures and other states and regions, city flags from across Europe and the US, historical flags like the Soviet Union's, pride flags, the flags of hundreds of peoples, separatist movements, micronations and international organizations. More are added category by category.

What if I don't know what kind of flag it is?

Leave it on "Not sure". If you do pick a kind, say a pride flag or a US state, those flags come first, and every other flag that fits is still listed below, in case it was something else.

Is it free?

Yes. No account needed, and nothing you pick leaves your device.
