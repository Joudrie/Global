Flag of Kosovo

Kosovo is one of the world's newest countries, having declared independence from Serbia in 2008 following a period of international administration after the 1998-99 conflict. It is recognised by over 100 UN member states and is a predominantly Albanian-populated territory with significant Serbian Orthodox cultural heritage, including medieval monasteries that are UNESCO World Heritage Sites. Pristina is its rapidly developing capital and largest city.

What the flag of Kosovo means

Kosovo's flag was chosen in 2008 at independence from a design competition, and it avoids the national symbols of both Albania and Serbia. It shows the country's outline in gold on blue, colours chosen to echo the flag of Europe. The six white stars stand for the main communities living in Kosovo: Albanians, Serbs, Turks, Gorani, Roma and Bosniaks.

More about the flag

Kosovo declared independence from Serbia in 2008, and about half of the member states of the United Nations recognise it; Serbia does not. The flag was designed by Muhamer Ibrahimi. Many Kosovo Albanians also fly the red flag of Albania with its black eagle, especially on Albanian Flag Day, 28 November, while the official flag appears on government buildings and at international events.

Flag history of Kosovo

Every flag that has flown over Kosovo, from today back through time.

2008–present

Flag of Kosovo

Adopted on 17 February 2008 with the declaration of independence — one of the world's newest national flags. It deliberately avoids Albanian and pan-Slavic colors, using a blue field with a gold map and six white stars for the main ethnic groups.

Related historical states

Empires, kingdoms and republics that once covered part of Kosovo.

1918–1941

Kingdom of Yugoslavia

The blue-white-red Pan-Slavic tricolor symbolized the union of Serbs, Croats, and Slovenes, flown by the Kingdom until the Axis invasion of 1941.

1945–1992

Socialist Yugoslavia

Tito's Partisans added a red, gold-bordered star to the tricolor; it marked the country's communist character until the federation's violent breakup in the 1990s.
