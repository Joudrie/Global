Globalio — Learn Every Flag in the World

A free flag & geography game with 51 ways to play: a daily Connections puzzle, quizzes, drawing and colouring games, map and border puzzles, and a language game. No sign-up required.

Learn the flags, capitals, borders and history of all 197 countries, plus more than 1,500 state and regional flags, hundreds of historical states and empires, and pride, indigenous, separatist and micronation flags.

Behind the games is a flag archive made by one developer: a page for every country explaining what its flag means, where its colours and symbols come from, and every flag that has flown there through history.
