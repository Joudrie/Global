Separatist & Autonomous flags

Flags of independence movements, breakaway states and autonomous regions. 142 flags, each with its story.

Abkhazia

The breakaway Georgian region's flag: seven green and white stripes with a red canton bearing an open hand and seven stars.

Acadia

The French tricolour with a gold star, flag of the Acadian people of Atlantic Canada.

Aceh (Free Aceh Movement)

A red field with white and black bands and a star and crescent — banner of the long Acehnese independence struggle in Indonesia.

Åland Islands

A red Nordic cross over a yellow one on blue — flag of the autonomous, Swedish-speaking Åland Islands of Finland.

Ambazonia

The blue-and-white flag with a dove and thirteen stars, declared by separatists in Cameroon's English-speaking regions.

Andalusia

Green-white-green with the coat of arms of Hercules — flag of Spain's most populous autonomous community and of Andalusian nationalism.

Artsakh (Nagorno-Karabakh)

The Armenian tricolour with a white stepped pattern at the fly, used by the self-declared Republic of Artsakh until its dissolution in 2024.

Asturias

A blue field with the golden Victory Cross — flag of the Principality of Asturias in northern Spain.

Azawad

The flag of the Tuareg state declared in northern Mali in 2012, which survived only months before being overrun.

Balochistan

A symbol of Baloch nationalism spanning Pakistan, Iran and Afghanistan.

Bangsamoro

Green with a yellow sword, crescent and seven-rayed star — flag of the Bangsamoro autonomous region of the southern Philippines.

Bashkortostan

Blue-white-green with a golden kurai flower — flag of the Bashkir republic within Russia.

Bavaria (Lozengy)

The white-and-blue diamond pattern of the German Free State of Bavaria, whose regionalist Bavaria Party has long sought independence.

Biafra

Red-black-green with a rising golden sun — flag of the short-lived Republic of Biafra (1967–70) and of present-day south-eastern Nigerian separatism.

Bougainville

Flag of the autonomous region of Papua New Guinea that voted ~98% for independence in 2019; the central upe is a traditional ceremonial headdress.

Brittany (Gwenn ha Du)

The 'White and Black': nine stripes for Brittany's historic dioceses and a field of black ermine spots. Designed in 1923, now the region's everyday flag.

Cabinda

The flag of the oil-rich Angolan exclave whose separatists have long sought independence, bearing the Simulambuco monument.

California Republic

The Bear Flag of the short-lived 1846 California Republic, now the state flag and a 'Calexit' symbol.

Canary Islands

White-blue-yellow vertical bands for the Spanish archipelago off Africa; independence groups fly a seven-green-star variant.

Cascadia (Doug Flag)

The blue-white-green 'Doug' flag with a Douglas fir, emblem of the Pacific Northwest bioregional movement spanning the US and Canada.

Catalonia (Senyera)

Four red bars on gold — one of Europe's oldest flags and the official banner of Catalonia.

Chechen Republic of Ichkeria

The green flag of the 1990s separatist Chechen republic, still flown by those who reject Russian rule over Chechnya.

Cornwall (St Piran's Flag)

A white cross on black, said to represent tin (white) running from ore (black). The banner of Cornish identity and the Cornish self-government movement.

Corsica (Moor's Head)

A black Moor's head with a white bandana on white — adopted by Corsican nationalist Pasquale Paoli in 1755 and a symbol of the island's autonomy movement.

Crimea

The flag of the contested Crimean peninsula, annexed by Russia in 2014.

Crimean Tatars

A golden tamga (clan seal) on light blue — emblem of the Indigenous Crimean Tatar people of the Black Sea peninsula.

Donetsk People's Republic

The black-blue-red banner of the Russian-backed breakaway in eastern Ukraine's Donbas.

East Turkestan (Kökbayraq)

A sky-blue flag with a white crescent and star, the banner of the Uyghur independence movement in China's Xinjiang region.

England (St George)

The red St George's cross on white — the flag of England within the United Kingdom.

Estelada Blava (Catalonia)

The Catalan independence flag: the red-and-yellow Senyera with a blue triangle and white star at the hoist, inspired by the Cuban and Puerto Rican flags.

Estelada Vermella (Catalonia)

The left-wing, socialist variant of the Catalan independence flag, with a yellow triangle and a red star.

Faroe Islands (Merkið)

A red-and-blue Nordic cross on white for the self-governing Faroe Islands, an autonomous nation within the Kingdom of Denmark.

Flanders (Flemish Lion)

A black lion on yellow — emblem of Dutch-speaking Flanders and the Flemish Movement within Belgium.

Frisia (Friesland)

Diagonal blue-and-white stripes strewn with red pompeblêden (water-lily leaves) — flag of the Frisian people of the Netherlands.

Galicia

A blue diagonal stripe on white — flag of the Celtic-rooted autonomous community of north-west Spain.

Hawaiian Kingdom

The flag of the former Kingdom of Hawai'i, central to the Native Hawaiian sovereignty movement.

Hong Kong (Colonial)

The pre-handover colonial ensign, waved by some pro-democracy and localist protesters.

Ikurriña (Basque Country)

Designed by the Arana brothers in 1894: a red field with a green saltire and white cross, now the official flag of the Basque Autonomous Community.

A red field with a plough and three white stripes — the former state flag of Indian-administered Jammu & Kashmir, abolished in 2019.

Kurdistan (Ala Rengîn)

Red-white-green stripes with a 21-ray golden sun (Roj). It is the official flag of Iraqi Kurdistan and a symbol for Kurds across the region.

Northern Cyprus (TRNC)

Padania

The green 'Sun of the Alps' on white, devised by Italy's Lega Nord for its proposed northern Italian state of Padania.

Puerto Rico

The single-starred flag of Puerto Rico — a US territory whose status is still debated; this flag was once banned as a separatist symbol.

Quebec (Fleurdelisé)

A white cross and four fleurs-de-lis on blue — flag of the French-speaking Canadian province and a banner of Québécois sovereignty.

Republic of the Rif
